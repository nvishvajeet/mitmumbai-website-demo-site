MIT Mumbai / Dr. Chetan K. Patil

Faculty

Dr. Chetan K. Patil

Associate Professor (Mechanical Engineering)

PhD: Noise, Vibration, and Harshness (NVH), MIT WPU, Pune

Dr. Chetan Kishor Patil is an accomplished Mechanical Engineer, researcher, and academician with over 13 years of teaching and research experience. He earned his Ph.D. in Noise and Vibrations from Dr. Vishwanath Karad MIT World Peace University, Pune, through a project sponsored by Kirloskar Oil Engines Ltd., Pune. His academic qualifications include an M.E. in Design Engineering from SRES College of Engineering, A. Nagar, and a B.E. in Mechanical Engineering from MIT Academy of Engineering, Pune.

His research expertise encompasses strength of material, robot kinematics and dynamics, machine design, vibrations, reliability engineering, and product development.

He has authored more than 20 research papers in reputed journals, contributed 2 book chapters, and guided 14 undergraduate & 3 postgraduate projects. He also holds multiple international and national patents across Germany, South Africa, and India, focusing on automotive safety systems, AI-based air conditioning, noise reduction panels, and reliability devices.

Research interests

  • Kinematics and Dynamics
