Faculty

Ms. Khushi Gupta

Assistant Professor of English

Master of Arts (MA) in English (IGNOU)

Ms. Gupta is an Assistant Professor in the Department of English. She holds a Master’s degree in English. She has a strong interest in literary theory and criticism. As a budding academic, she is eager to contribute to teaching and research in higher education. Her areas of interest include psychoanalysis, modern literature and communication. She believes in creating an interactive and student-friendly learning environment and is motivated to guide students in both academic and personal growth.

Research interests

  • Psychoanalysis
  • Literary Theory and Criticism
  • Committed English Literature Postgraduate
