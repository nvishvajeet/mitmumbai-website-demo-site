MIT Mumbai / Dr. Himanshi Gupta

Faculty

Dr. Himanshi Gupta

Assistant Professor of Mechanical Engineering

PhD: IC Engines & Alternative Fuels, MNNIT Allahabad

Dr. Himanshi Gupta is a mechanical engineer specializing in internal combustion engines and alternative fuels, with research expertise in combustion, performance, and emission characteristics of diesel engines fueled with renewable biodiesel blends. She earned her Ph.D. in Mechanical Engineering from the Department of Mechanical Engineering, Motilal Nehru National Institute of Technology (MNNIT), Allahabad. She holds an M.Tech. in Thermal Engineering from Pandit Deendayal Energy University (PDEU), Gandhinagar, and a B.E. in Mechanical Engineering from Gujarat Technological University (GTU), Gujarat. She is currently serving as an Assistant Professor at MAEER's MIT, Mumbai, in the Department of First Year Engineering.

Research interests

  • Internal Combustion Engines
  • Alternative and Renewable Fuels
