About

Completed Masters in 3 Subjects, that is, English, History and Political Science, Rupsa mukherjee is an expert in Englsih communication. Having trained students for competitive exams, she is an experienced faculty for teaching communication in India and abroad. She also has an experience for conducting debates, group discussions and story-telling competitions and programs. Having expertise in cultural activities as well, she helps in the all round development of the students and make them prepared for different practical communication situations.
