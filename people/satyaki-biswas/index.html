MIT Mumbai / Dr. Satyaki Biswas

Faculty

Dr. Satyaki Biswas

Assistant Professor of Basic Electrical and Electronics Engineering

PhD: Engineering (Specialization: Power and Energy Systems), from the National Institute of Technology Silchar, India.

Dr. Satyaki Biswas holds a Ph.D. in Electrical Engineering from the National Institute of Technology Silchar, specializing in power systems, renewable energy, microgrids, and electric vehicles. His research focuses on microgrid, smart grid, sustainable energy management, system reliability, and electric vehicles. He has published multiple papers in reputed journals and conferences, contributing to advancements in microgrid design and smart grid technologies. He is passionate about innovative teaching, research excellence, and practical energy solutions, while also pursuing interests in sports and creative arts.

Research interests

  • Microgrid
  • Renewable energy sources
  • Energy Management System
  • Electric Vehicle
  • Reliability
  • Environmental and economic constraints.
