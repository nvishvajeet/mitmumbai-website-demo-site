Mr. Sudhanwa M. Kulkarni

PhD: Mechanical Engineering, BITS Pilani, K. K. Birla Goa Campus (Thesis Submitted, Awaiting Viva)

About

Assistant Professor, Mechanical Engineering Department, Maharashtra Institute of Technology College of Engineering, Pune (2013 – 2018)

Assistant Professor, Mechanical Engineering Department, Maharashtra Institute of Technology World Peace University, Pune (2018 – 2020)

Teaching Assistant, BITS Pilani, Goa Campus (2020 – 2025)

Research interests

  • Vehicle Safety & Structural Crashworthiness
  • Crash-box Design & Optimization
  • Impact Modeling
  • Friction Stir Welding/Processing

Selected publications

  1. Sudhanwa Mahesh Kulkarni, Kiran D. Mali, Dhananjay M. Kulkarni, N. Iniyan Thiruselvam, Novel thermo-mechanical triggers processed by friction stir to enhance the crashworthiness of an automobile crash-box, Engineering Failure Analysis , Vol. 162, 2024.
  2. Sudhanwa M. Kulkarni, Priyal V., Kiran D. Mali, Dhananjay M. Kulkarni, Selection of an appropriate material for a crash-box in multi-attribute group decision-making environment using R-method, International Journal of Crashworthiness , 28(5), 2022.
  3. S. M. Kulkarni, Priyal Vemu, K. D. Mali, D. M. Kulkarni, Effect of geometric irregularities induced during manufacturing of a crash-box on its crashworthiness performance, Materials Today: Proceedings , 57(2), 2022.
  4. Kulkarni, S. M., Mali, K. D., Kulkarni, D. M. et al., Influence of friction stir processing parameters on the mechanical properties of aluminium tubes, Interactions , 245, 380 (2024).
  5. Analytical and experimental crashworthiness investigations of laterally thickness graded cylindrical crash-boxes, Experimental Mechanics [Under Review].
  6. Crashworthiness Performance Optimization of Bio-Inspired Thickness Graded Circular Energy Absorbers, International Journal of Solids and Structures [Under Review].

Conference papers

  1. Effect of imperfection and various contact algorithms on the simulation of quasi-static collapse of thin-walled energy absorbers using ABAQUS/Explicit, ICRAMERD 2022, Springer LNME.
  2. Energy Absorption Characteristics of Aluminium Alloy Tubes Subjected to Quasi-static Axial Load, WMVC 2022, Springer MMS.
  3. Crashworthiness performance of cylindrical crash-boxes with varying rib configurations, ICMMM 2025, Bangkok, Thailand.
