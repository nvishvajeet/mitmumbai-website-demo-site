MIT Mumbai / Mr. Akshay Sudam Karad

Faculty

Mr. Akshay Sudam Karad

Workshop Superintendent, Department of First Year Engineering at MAEERS MIT Thane

Akshay S. Karad is presently working as a workshop superintendent in the Department of First Year Engineering at MAEERS MIT Thane. He did his post-graduation in Mechanical Engineering from Dr. Vishwanath Karad MIT World Peace University (MITWPU), Pune in 2023 and has an experience of one year at Defence Institute Advanced Technology (DIAT), Pune as an intern. He has also worked at MITWPU as a Research associate for one year and three months. He has published four research articles in SCOUPS.

Research interests

  • Composite materials
  • Additive Manufacturing
