MIT Mumbai / Dr. Abhijeet Ghogare

Faculty

Dr. Abhijeet Ghogare

Assistant Professor (Mechanical Engineering)

PhD: Computational Fluids Dynamics (CFD) in Thermal and Fluids Engineering, NIT Raipur

Dr. Abhijeet Ghogare was born in Nagpur, Maharashtra, India. He received his B.E. in Mechanical Engineering with First Division from Rashtrasant Tukadoji Maharaj Nagpur University (RTMNU), Nagpur in 2015. He completed his M.Tech in Thermal and Fluids Engineering from NIT Manipur in 2019. He obtained his Ph.D. in Mechanical Engineering from NIT Raipur in 2024. After PhD, he worked for 1 year on the DST/SERB project on helicopter dynamics at MANIT Bhopal.

He has published 8 research articles (5 SCI-indexed, 3 Scopus-indexed), contributed 1 book chapter, and presented 2 papers in international conferences. His work has received 311 citations with an h-index of 5 and an i10-index of 5 . He is an active member of ISHRAE.

Research interests

  • Computational Fluid Dynamics
  • Thermal Comfort
  • Green Hydrogen / Biodiesels
