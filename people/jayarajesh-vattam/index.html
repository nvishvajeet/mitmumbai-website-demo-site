Mr. Jayarajesh Vattam

Assistant Professor in Electronics Engineering

About

Experienced academician with over 15+ years of teaching experience in Electronics & Electrical Engineering, including 11 years as Head of Department and 3 years as Dean Academics. Currently pursuing Ph.D. from NIT Jalandhar and holding an M.Tech degree in Power Systems from NIT Calicut. Skilled in teaching diverse subjects across Electronics, Electrical & Telecommunication domains, with expertise in Verilog, SystemVerilog, UVM, and industry-standard protocols (AXI, AHB, APB). Published multiple research papers, authored books, and granted patents in the areas of IoT and AI-based energy management. Proven leadership in academic administration, curriculum design, accreditation processes (NAAC, NBA), and project guidance. Dedicated to fostering innovation and delivering quality education through a blend of technical proficiency and management expertise.

Research interests

  • Electric & Hybrid Electric Vehicles, Battery Management Systems
  • Digital Signal Processing
