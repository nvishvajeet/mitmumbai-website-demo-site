  • 1.10.2025
  • MIT Mumbai: First Year Engineering Student Induction Program – 2025

MIT Mumbai conducted the Induction Program for newly admitted First Year Engineering students for the academic year 2024–25. The event was graced by Mr. Sanjay Bhatia (IAS), Head of Lokayukta, Maharashtra State; Former Chairman, Mumbai Port Trust & Indian Ports Association , who addressed the gathering and guided students on their transition into engineering education. The program also featured insightful addresses by Director, Prof. Jyoti A. Dhakne; Principal, Dr. Vilas Nitnaware; and Registrar, Dr. Chetan K. Patil , providing students and parents with an overview of institutional policies, academic processes, and the opportunities available at MIT Mumbai.

  • Engineers’ Day Celebration at MIT Mumbai – 2025

MIT Mumbai celebrated Engineers’ Day with great enthusiasm, honoring the contributions and spirit of engineering excellence. The event was graced by the presence of Hon’ble Director, Prof. Jyoti A.   • TechSphere 2025 – First Year Engineering Project Exhibition-cum-Competition

MIT Mumbai organized TechSphere 2025 , a First Year Engineering Project Exhibition-cum-Competition , showcasing innovative ideas and creative engineering solutions developed by FY students. The event provided a platform for students to present their projects, demonstrate practical applications of engineering concepts, and enhance their problem-solving skills.
