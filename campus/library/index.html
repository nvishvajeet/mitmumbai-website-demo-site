Central Library

Library

  • MIT-Library

The Central Library at MIT Mumbai curates and provides comprehensive access to extensive scholarly resources, facilitating intellectual stimulation, research advancement, and creative innovation. It offers information services and platforms designed to enhance knowledge acquisition, skill enrichment, and academic rigor among faculty, students, and staff. It aims to expand intellectual horizons, foster critical thinking, and strengthen competencies essential for addressing contemporary global challenges.

MIT, Mumbai offers an extensive array of library resources comprising comprehensive physical collections and advanced digital databases, enabling students and researchers to thoroughly explore a broad range of academic disciplines.

Our library maintains a comprehensive collection of 24 essential textbook titles for first-year engineering students, totaling 256 copies. These resources span Robotics (“Introduction to Industrial Robotics”), Mathematics (Higher Engineering Mathematics; Advanced Engineering Mathematics), Physics (Engineering Physics; Concepts of Modern Engineering Physics), Chemistry (Engineering Chemistry; A Textbook of Engineering Chemistry), and specialized subjects such as Numerical Methods with MATLAB, Machine Drawing, Verilog HDL, Data Structures Using C, Blockchain, and Cloud Computing.

  • Issued by leading publishers: Pearson, McGraw Hill, Wiley, Oxford
  • Provides equitable access to learning resources
  • Ensures convenient accessibility for all students
  • Offers up-to-date and authoritative content
  • Supports academic excellence
  • Promotes practical skills development and research readiness

Our Central Library offers eight key titles on the Indian Knowledge System (IKS) for Semester II, totaling 40 copies. The collection features “Introduction to Indian Knowledge System: Concepts and Applications” (Mahadevan, Bhat & Nagendra), “India’s Glorious Scientific Tradition” (Soni), “Indian Art: Forms, Concerns and Development” (Chattopadhyaya & Goswamy), and “Indian Knowledge Systems” (Kapoor & Singh).

  • Includes “Comprehensive Human Physiology” by Pandey, covering foundational biological systems.

Features “Aayurved ka Itihas evam Parichay” by Shukla, introducing the history and principles of Ayurveda.

Offers “IKS: The Knowledge System of Bharata” by Chauhan, exploring India's traditional knowledge systems.

Provides “Traditional Knowledge System in India” by Jha, offering insights into indigenous Indian sciences.

  • Supports interdisciplinary learning, integrating science, history, and cultural studies.
  • Encourages critical inquiry and research into India’s intellectual and philosophical heritage.

The Central Library subscribes to a range of reputed scientific journals to support research and academics.

Indian Journal of Pure and Applied Mathematics – Published quarterly by the Indian National Science Academy.

  • Indian Journal of Traditional Knowledge – Published monthly by NISCAIR, Delhi.
  • Indian Journal of Chemistry – Published monthly by NISCAIR, Delhi.
  • Indian Journal of Pure and Applied Physics – Published monthly by NISCAIR, Delhi.

Indian Journal of Engineering and Material Science – A bi-monthly publication by NISCAIR, Delhi.

International Journal of Physics Research and Development – A bi-annual, Scopus-indexed journal by IAEME, Chennai.

These journals provide updated and credible content across disciplines like mathematics, physics, chemistry, engineering, and traditional knowledge systems.
