Fraud Alert

  • Important Notice
  • Fraud Alert & Public Disclaimer

At MIT Mumbai , we have recently observed certain unauthorized advertisements, online posts, and websites circulating misleading information related to admissions and so-called “management quota” opportunities. These platforms are unlawfully using the name, logo, photographs, and other identity elements of our institution without any approval or authorization.

It has come to our attention that some individuals and organizations are falsely claiming to represent or be associated with MIT Mumbai. Such entities are creating confusion among students and parents by publishing copied or manipulated content resembling information available on our official website.

MIT Mumbai hereby clarifies that the institute does not authorize or engage any external agents, consultants, agencies, or third-party organizations for admission activities or student enrollment in any course or program offered by the institute.

Students, parents, and the general public are strongly advised to avoid interacting with or making any payments to such unauthorized persons, websites, or agencies claiming to provide admissions on behalf of MIT Mumbai. The institute shall not be held responsible for any loss, damage, or consequences arising from dealings with such fraudulent entities.

Any person choosing to engage with these misleading platforms or unauthorized representatives shall do so entirely at their own risk.

For genuine admission-related updates, official announcements, and authentic information, please refer only to our official website: www.mitmumbai.edu.in
