Academic Distinction

Our institution has earned prestigious academic recognitions that enrich learning experiences and provide students with exposure to nationally acclaimed platforms.

  • MAEER’s Virtual Labs

MAEER’s institute provides access to Virtual Labs of IIT Bombay , enabling students to perform experiments in a virtual environment. This initiative allows learners to explore advanced experiments and concepts beyond the prescribed syllabus , strengthening practical understanding and innovation.

  • NPTEL Local Chapter – MAEER’s MIT Thane

MAEER’s MIT Thane has been granted the status of an NPTEL Local Chapter . This recognition enables students to enroll in training and certification programs conducted by IITs , offered through online and virtual learning modes, enhancing academic and professional competencies.
